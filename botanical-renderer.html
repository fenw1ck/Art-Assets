How this works
Enter what you know. Leave unknown fields blank or choose “Research needed.” Add source links and photographs if you have them. The brief separates supplied information from questions still requiring research. Export it as JSON to hand to the renderer or a research pass. Existing artwork is treated as the starting reference and must never be overwritten.

1. Plant identity and existing artwork

Images are embedded in the exported JSON. Use clear views of the whole plant, leaves, stems, flowers and fruit where possible.

What if I do not know the botanical terms?

Leave the field blank. The renderer should treat it as a research question, not guess. Photos and ordinary observations such as “the leaves come in pairs” or “fruit hangs below the flower” are useful evidence.

7. Review and export

The exported record keeps user-supplied facts separate from unanswered research. It is a handoff brief; this static website does not itself browse sources or execute the Python renderer.
