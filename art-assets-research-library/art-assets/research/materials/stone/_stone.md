# Stone

## Parts (what to record for every stone)

- Ground colour, and its range.
- Grain: fine, medium or coarse. Speckle from the minerals.
- Structure: bedding or banding (sedimentary), veins (marble), clasts (breccia, conglomerate), fossils.
- Finish: polished, honed, sawn, or tooled.
- Ageing: patina, erosion of soft beds, cracking, staining, and polish where feet or hands touch.
