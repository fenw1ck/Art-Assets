# Metal

## Distinguishing features

- **Wrought iron:** hammered or rolled while hot, so its surface is rough and fibrous. Old pieces show few hammer marks but softened edges and irregular pitting. Finished iron is dark grey to black, with some silver showing on high points and red tones in the forge scale.
- **Rust** forms where unprotected iron meets air and moisture: orange-brown, in pits and along edges.
- **Brass and bronze:** gold to brown. Aged, they darken to brown-black with the gold showing through where rubbed. Outdoors over long periods they turn blue-green (verdigris).

## Rendering notes

- High contrast; a thin bright highlight along the upper edge of each strap facing the light.
- Rivet heads: small domes with a glint.
- Wear: the dark patina rubs off on raised edges (lighter, more silver). Rust and verdigris collect in recesses and low down.
