# Villamayor sandstone

## Distinguishing features

- An arkose (feldspar-rich sandstone) from the Middle Eocene, fine- to medium-grained: quartz and feldspar with clay minerals and iron oxides.
- White to ochre and red when fresh. It develops an ochreous, golden patina, which gives Salamanca its golden colour.
- Quarried at Villamayor de Armuña, about 7 km north of Salamanca. Used for the cathedrals, the Plaza Mayor and the university façade.
- Soft and easily carved when wet, which made the fine Plateresque carving possible. It also weathers easily, so lower courses were sometimes built in the harder Salamanca sandstone.
- A Global Heritage Stone Resource (IUGS, 2017).

## Rendering notes

- Fine grain speckle and faint bedding.
- Golden patina strongest on weathered, sunlit faces; paler where freshly cut or restored.
- Soft edges and rounded wear, crumbling on low courses.
