# Wear, weathering and decline

## Parts (from biggest to smallest)

1. **Structural:** cracks, chipped or missing pieces, collapse.
2. **Environmental:** water stains, moss, bleaching, patina, rust.
3. **Surface:** edge wear on convex edges and corners, scuffs, and polish where things are touched or walked on.

## Rules

- Wear tells a story and follows causes: feet polish the middle of a corridor, water stains run down below windows and joints, edges chip first.
- Write the rules down and apply them the same way everywhere. Random wear reads as noise.
- One `wear` amount (0 = new, 1 = ruined) drives all three layers.
- Plants: see the declining and dead states in `subjects/plants/_plants.md`.
