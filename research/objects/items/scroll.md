# Scroll

## Parts

- A **roll** of papyrus, parchment or paper with writing. Papyrus sheets are two layers of pith strips laid crosswise, which gives the surface its fine **cross-hatching**; rolls were wound with the vertical fibres outside.
- **Ends:** the spiral of rolled layers shows at each end.
- **Tie:** a cord or ribbon round the middle, sometimes with a **seal** pressed onto the tie (the "roll-tied and sealed" form).

## Distinguishing features (the game's scrolls)

- Antiqued: warm parchment colour, darker worn ends, foxing spots, a slightly torn outer edge.
- **Ribbon colour varies.** Each of the game's six scrolls has its own: Circles red, Straightedge blue, Angles green, Parallels violet, Equal Areas gold, Squares black. Any other scroll picks one of eight by its seed. A red wax seal stamped with the triangle sits on the tie.
- State: rolled (a consumable: used once, then gone).
