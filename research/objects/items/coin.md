# Coin

## Parts

- **Flan:** the struck blank, never quite round on a hand-struck coin.
- **Obverse / reverse:** the two faces; the image on a face is its **type**, the empty space around it the **field**.
- **Legend:** the writing, usually running round the edge in a circle. Here: ΕΥΚΛΕΙΔΟΥ ΣΤΟΙΧΕΙΑ ("Euclid's Elements").
- **Beading:** a border of dots round the rim (a ring repeat).
- **Crest:** the game's seal, a triangle in a circle, raised.
- Metals: gold (AV), silver (AR), bronze/copper (AE).

## States

- **fresh**; **worn** (relief rubbed smooth, low contrast); **rusty** (the coating that builds up while buried: green verdigris on bronze, black toning on silver, rust on iron; gold only dulls); **muddy** (patches of dried earth).
