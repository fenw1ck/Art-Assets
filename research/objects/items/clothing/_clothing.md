# Clothing

## Parts

- Worn in **named slots**, one item each: **boots, cape, rings, hat, gloves, shirt, trousers**. A worn item leaves the bag (it doesn't take a bag slot) and shows in its slot.
- The wardrobe uses the game's **gem-and-hollow** idea: each slot is a carved hollow, and an item is dragged into the hollow that fits it.
- Ancient Greek dress for the look: the **chiton** (a linen tunic), sandals and boots of leather; Greek footwear ranged from sandals to slippers to boots, and leather was the main material.
