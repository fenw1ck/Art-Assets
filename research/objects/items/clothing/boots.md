# Boots and sandals

## Distinguishing features (the game's boots)

| Upgrade | Item | Ancient model | Look | Walking speed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scholar's Sandals | **krepides** | a leather sole, multi-strapped with crisscrossing straps up the ankle | ×1.25 |
| 2 | Wayfarer's Boots | **endromis** | a short hunting boot with an open front, a long tongue and laces | ×1.5 |
| 3 | Winged Sandals | **talaria** (Hermes' winged sandals) | gold sole and straps, small white wings at the ankle | ×1.8 |
| — | Greaves of the Long Stair | **knemides** (greaves) | bronze shin guards, etched with a staircase of squares | ornamental |

- Each speed upgrade is found once, hidden in a hall, in order; the shop also sells them.
- Drawn as a pair in side view, the back one a little behind and fainter.
