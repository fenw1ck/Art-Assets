# Backpack

## Distinguishing features

- **The backpack is the inventory's size.** In Stardew Valley the bag starts at 12 slots and two upgrades are bought from the general store: the red Large Pack (24) and the blue Deluxe Pack (36). Palia starts at 16 slots and sells larger backpacks the same way. Each size is a new-looking pack, not a recolour.
- **Bought once, never carried as an item.** Buying the next pack swaps it in and moves everything over.
- **Sizes here:** satchel 16 (start), Rucksack 24 (40 coins), Expedition Pack 32 (90 coins).
