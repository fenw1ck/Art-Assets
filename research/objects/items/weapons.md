# Weapons

## Distinguishing features

- **Close quarters only** (the rooms are small): knife (fast, short reach), sword (strongest), staff (longest reach, wooden with metal shoe and cap).
- **Three material strengths:** bronze, iron, starmetal (the same tiers as the axes). Starmetal has a pale blue sheen and glints; a starmetal staff carries a glowing blue stone.
- **Wear states for a consumable weapon:** perfect, damaged (nicked edge, scratches, a darker blade; a crack in a staff), broken (the blade snapped with the tip lying beside it; the staff split in two). Each hit uses one of its uses (bronze 18, iron 32, starmetal 55).
- Drawn on a diagonal, point up and to the right; grip wrapped in leather, a round pommel and a crossguard in the blade's metal.
