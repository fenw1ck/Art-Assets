# Bed

## Distinguishing features (the six styles drawn)

- **Four-post:** tall turned posts rising from each corner, finials on top.
- **Canopy:** a four-poster whose posts are joined at the top by crossbeams, hung with a valance and drapes.
- **Tree of life:** an arched wooden headboard carved with a tree: roots, trunk and a branching crown in a circle.
- **Art deco:** a lacquered, stepped headboard with a sunburst fan in gold lines, and a matching footboard.
- **Bunk:** one twin bed stacked over another, a guard rail on top, a ladder at the foot.
- **Japanese futon:** no frame: a mattress (shikibuton) and duvet (kakebuton) laid on a tatami mat, folded away by day.
- Twin mattress about 38 × 75 in (about 1 × 1.9 m); frames about 2.05 m long. Drawn front view (from the foot, with the headboard behind) or side view.
