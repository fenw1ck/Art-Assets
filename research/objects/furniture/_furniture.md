# Furniture

## Parts

- Pieces for the Euclid game's room types (library now; bedroom, pantry, bathroom and kitchen later). The game's halls use 1 unit = about 1 m (doors about 2 m tall), so furniture is drawn at real sizes.
- Wood: see `materials/wood/_wood.md`. Light from the upper left: the top and left edges lightest.

## Distinguishing features (standard sizes)

- **Bookshelf (bookcase):** 30–48 in wide, 12–14 in deep, 72–84 in tall; shelves every 10–12 in. Drawn as a built-in case with a cornice, a plinth, and books of mixed heights and colours (gilt bands on the spines), a few leaning, a few gaps.
- **Armchair:** 26–40 in wide and deep; seat 16–20 in high; 30–40 in or more tall overall. Drawn as a rolled-arm club chair on short legs, front or side view.
- **Side (end) table:** 24–26 in tall, 18–24 in wide, about the height of a sofa arm. Drawn round, on a turned pedestal and tripod foot, with a book and sometimes a lit lamp.
