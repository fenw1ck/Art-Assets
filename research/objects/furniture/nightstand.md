# Nightstand, nightlight and mobile

## Distinguishing features

- **Nightstand:** the library's side table made into a small cabinet: about 0.48 m wide and 0.58 m tall, two drawers with brass knobs, short legs, a top that overhangs a little. Stands beside the head of a bed.
- **Nightlight** on the nightstand: a frosted **cylinder**, a glowing **sphere** on a little base, or a **jar of fireflies** (glass jar, lid, lights blinking on and off).
- **Mobile** hanging from the ceiling over a bed: a crossbar turning slowly, figures on threads of different lengths, each turning on its own. Themes: **propeller planes** (a yellow Cub, a red biplane, a floatplane, a white Cessna with a stripe), **woodland creatures** (fox, rabbit, hedgehog, owl), **space** (stars, a ringed planet, the moon, planets).
