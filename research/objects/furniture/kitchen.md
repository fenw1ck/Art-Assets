# Kitchen

## Distinguishing features

- **The range** (a cast-iron cookstove, the heart of a 19th-century kitchen): a heavy black iron box on short legs, a firebox with a grate behind a small door, an oven beside it (hottest next to the firebox), round hob plates on top for pots and a kettle, a stove pipe up the wall, a brass rail; often a tiled splashback.
- **Kitchen table:** a thick scrubbed top on turned legs, a bench; **island:** a block (butcher-block) top on painted cupboards, optionally a rack of hanging pans.
- In the game the kitchen is a room type: a stove always (the oven is where ingredients are cooked) and either a table or an island.
