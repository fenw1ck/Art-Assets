# Cooking fire and fuel

## Distinguishing features

- **Campfire:** a ring of grey stones, split logs crossed in a cone, layered flames (orange, gold, pale yellow) and rising sparks; unlit, grey ash and a few embers.
- **Cauldron:** a round-bellied black iron pot with a rolled rim and a bail handle, hung from a tripod of iron rods over the fire; bubbling and steaming while cooking.
- **Fuel:** a tied bundle of split logs (firewood); a pile of twigs and chopped vine (kindling). Five kindling make one firewood.
- **The cooking check** follows Cult of the Lamb's cooking fire (press when the moving slider is in the green section) and Disney Dreamlight Valley's stove: one range per ingredient; simple dishes get a wide range, many-ingredient dishes several narrower ones.
- **Cooked food art** (`items.js` dish): soups and stews in a bowl with steam; roasts and single cooked vegetables on a plate with char marks; burnt food a black lump with smoke.
