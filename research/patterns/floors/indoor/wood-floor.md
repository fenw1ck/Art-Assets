# Wood floor

## Distinguishing features

- Planks laid straight, running along the room, with end joints staggered at random so they never line up; joints in neighbouring rows kept well apart (no "H" joints).
- Standard planks are about 5 in (0.13 m) wide; drawn 0.12–0.2 m. Species palettes: oak, walnut, pine, cherry.

## Construction order

Dark seam fill; columns of planks; each plank a random length (0.9–2.5 m), its tone a blend of the palette, three wavering grain lines, sometimes a knot, a light-to-shade gradient across it; an end joint moved if it would fall within two plank widths of the last row's.
