# Stone floor

## Distinguishing features

- Cut flagstones in rows of mixed lengths (random ashlar), so no two stones match and joints do not line up; old floors were laid this way with mixed widths, which gives them their character.
- Palettes: limestone (cream-grey), sandstone (honey), slate (blue-grey). Grout darker than the stone.

## Construction order

Grout fill; rows 0.4–0.75 m deep; stones 0.45–1.25 m long, each a blend of two palette tones, lit from the upper left, speckled; an occasional hairline crack; a paler, worn band down the middle where people walk.
