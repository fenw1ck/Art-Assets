# Carpet

## Distinguishing features

- A rug laid over a stone or wood floor: guard stripes, a main border with a running motif, a field with a small lattice, a central medallion and corner pieces, fringe at both ends. Four palettes (red/navy, blue/red, green/gold, plum/teal), all with ivory and gold.
- Sizes: a runner down a corridor, a large carpet in a library, a rug in the middle of a bedroom.
