# Basilisk

## Distinguishing features

- In Greek and Roman writers a deadly serpent ("little king") that killed by touch, breath or gaze. The game uses the stare: a basilisk stuns as it bites.
- Drawn: an olive serpent coiled in three loops, the neck raised, a pale belly stripe, a three-pointed golden crest like a crown, yellow eyes; threatening, a beam of gaze from the eye.
