# Manticore

## Distinguishing features

- A winged Persian monster (described by Greek writers) with a man's head, a lion's body and a tail that throws spines; later accounts give it bat or dragon wings and three rows of teeth.
- Drawn: a red-gold lion's body, a maned face (human-like), dark bat wings, a segmented scorpion tail with a black sting.
