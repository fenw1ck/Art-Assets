# Hydra

## Distinguishing features

- The Lernaean Hydra: a many-headed water serpent (often nine heads) of the swamps of Lerna, killed by Heracles in his second labour.
- Drawn: a green scaled body with a pale belly and five necks fanning up from it, each with a small snake's head; threatening, all mouths open with forked red tongues; wounded, some heads drooping.
