# Harpy

## Distinguishing features

- Part bird, part woman: a woman's face and head, a bird's body, wings and taloned feet (Greek vases show them so, for example a hydria from Vulci, about 530–500 BCE). The personification of storm winds, snatchers.
- Drawn feathered to the neck (scale-like feather rows), long dark hair, golden legs and talons, hovering a little off the ground unless wounded.
