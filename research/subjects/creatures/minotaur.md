# Minotaur

## Distinguishing features

- The body of a man and the head of a bull; kept in the Labyrinth of Crete, killed by Theseus.
- Drawn: bull head with pale curved horns and a brass nose ring, a heavy tan torso, a leather kilt with a gold belt, dark furred legs and hooves. Threatening: one fist raised, mouth open.
