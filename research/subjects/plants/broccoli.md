# Broccoli (Brassica oleracea Italica Group)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Upright, branched biennial herb; cultivated broccoli is harvested for its enlarged immature branched inflorescence.
- **Leaves:** Alternate blue-green leaves, usually broad and lobed with a waxy surface and prominent midrib; leaf stalks attach along a thick central stem.
- **Stem and branching:** Strong upright central stem with lateral branches; the terminal and axillary inflorescences form compact clusters of unopened flower buds.
- **Flowers:** Dense green clusters of immature flower buds; if allowed to bolt, branches elongate into racemes of yellow four-petalled flowers.
- **Fruit or harvest organ:** Narrow siliques develop after flowering; the edible broccoli head is not fruit but immature inflorescence.

## Renderer construction rules

Make the edible head as many tiny tightly packed green buds on branched stalks, with a thick central stem and large leaves below. Keep the head visibly granular/branched rather than a single smooth ball. A flowering stage should open into yellow cruciform flowers on elongated racemes.
