# Carrot family

## Parts

- Long leaf stalks rise from a crown at the ground.
- When the plant bolts, tall stalks carry flowers in umbels, often umbels of smaller umbels. This is the self-repeating pattern.

## Distinguishing features

- **Parsley:** leaves cut into sharp-toothed leaflets of three lobes.
- **Cilantro:** lower leaves have rounded, toothed lobes; upper leaves are feathery; white umbels.
- **Dill:** leaves are fine threads; yellow umbels.
