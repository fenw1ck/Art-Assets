# French marigold (Tagetes patula)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Compact, erect, aromatic annual, usually branching from lower and middle stem nodes.
- **Leaves:** Opposite lower leaves (upper may be alternate), pinnately divided into narrow elliptic or lanceolate leaflets with toothed margins and visible oil glands.
- **Stem and branching:** Many short erect or spreading branches; flower heads terminate long peduncles, often solitary at branch tips.
- **Flowers:** Asteraceae capitulum with outer ray florets (yellow, orange, red-brown or bicoloured) around many small disc florets; involucre forms a short cylinder below head.
- **Fruit or propagules:** Dark narrow achene with a small pappus of scales/bristles.

## Renderer construction rules

Use finely divided aromatic foliage, not broad simple leaves. Keep branching compact and repeated from nodes. Each flower is one composite head on a distinct peduncle; show rays around a central disc, and retain the involucre beneath. Cultivar may be single or densely double.
