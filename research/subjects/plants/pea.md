# Garden pea (Pisum sativum)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Scrambling, glaucous annual climber with slender stems.
- **Leaves:** Alternate pinnate compound leaves with one to several opposite pairs of ovate leaflets; large leafy stipules at the leaf base; terminal leaflet pair modified into branched tendrils.
- **Stem and branching:** Slender stems extend and lean; branched tendrils at the end of each compound leaf grasp supports. This is leaf-derived tendril climbing, not twining stems.
- **Flowers:** One to three papilionaceous flowers on short axillary racemes; white to pink or purple; flower stalks arise from leaf axils.
- **Fruit:** Elongated legume pod, several seeds arranged in a single row; pods follow the axillary flower positions.

## Renderer construction rules

Build a delicate scrambling stem with large stipules at nodes. Each compound leaf carries paired leaflets and ends in a branched tendril; tendrils should visibly coil around supports. Place flowers and pods in axils, not at the tendril tips.
