# Greek oregano (Origanum vulgare subsp. hirtum)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Low perennial aromatic subshrub with upright flowering stems.
- **Leaves:** Opposite small ovate leaves, usually densely hairy and strongly aromatic.
- **Stem and branching:** Square stems branch from crown; terminal flowering panicles rise above foliage.
- **Flowers:** Small white bilabiate flowers in dense terminal clusters.
- **Fruit/harvest:** Small nutlets.

## Renderer construction rules

Distinguish from common oregano by typically hairier foliage and white flowers; retain opposite leaf pairs and terminal clusters.
