# Snake plant (Dracaena trifasciata (syn. Sansevieria trifasciata))

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Rhizomatous evergreen succulent herb forming upright fans or clumps of sword-like leaves.
- **Leaves:** Thick, stiff, erect linear-lanceolate leaves with sharp tips; transversely banded dark and pale green; margins may be yellow in some cultivars.
- **Stem and branching:** Very short stem at soil level; fleshy creeping rhizome connects separate leaf fans and produces offsets. Leaves arise in small clusters from rhizome.
- **Flowers:** Tall erect raceme with small clusters of greenish-white fragrant tubular flowers; flowering is occasional indoors.
- **Fruit or propagules:** Small orange berries after flowering.

## Renderer construction rules

Construct individual stiff blades rising directly from the basal rhizome in distinct fans; taper each blade to a point and show transverse banding. Do not draw a branched woody stem or broad leaf canopy. Offsets arise from underground rhizome.
