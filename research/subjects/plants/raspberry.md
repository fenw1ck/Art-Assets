# Red raspberry (Rubus idaeus)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Perennial rootstock producing biennial canes; first-year primocanes are vegetative, second-year floricanes fruit and then die.
- **Leaves:** Alternate compound leaves, usually 3–5 leaflets; serrated margins, pale or whitish hairy undersides; terminal leaflet often larger.
- **Stem and branching:** Multiple upright to arching canes from crown/root suckers. Floricanes develop lateral fruiting branches from leaf axils; canes are prickly in many cultivars.
- **Flowers:** Small white five-petalled flowers in clusters on lateral branches of floricanes.
- **Fruit or propagules:** Aggregate of many drupelets, each containing a tiny stone; ripe fruit separates from the receptacle, leaving a hollow centre.

## Renderer construction rules

Show both cane ages only when illustrating a mature planting: tall first-year leafy primocanes and older floricanes with lateral fruiting branches. Attach pinnate leaves alternately. Fruit clusters occur on floricane laterals, not uniformly along every cane.
