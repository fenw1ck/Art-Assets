# Lemon thyme (Thymus × citriodorus)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Low aromatic woody-based subshrub forming a compact spreading mound.
- **Leaves:** Very small opposite oval leaves, often lemon-scented; cultivar variegation may occur.
- **Stem and branching:** Fine woody stems branch densely near base; short upright flowering tips.
- **Flowers:** Tiny pink-lilac bilabiate flowers in terminal or upper axillary whorls.
- **Fruit/harvest:** Four nutlets.

## Renderer construction rules

Build a low fine-textured mound with tiny opposite leaves; do not enlarge leaves to common thyme scale. Variegation is cultivar-dependent.
