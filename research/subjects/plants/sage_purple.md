# Purple sage (Salvia officinalis (purple-leaved cultivar group))

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Woody-based aromatic subshrub with upright square flowering shoots.
- **Leaves:** Opposite oblong-ovate rugose leaves; purple pigmentation varies with cultivar, light and temperature.
- **Stem and branching:** Opposite node pairs on square stems; woody basal branching.
- **Flowers:** Blue-violet bilabiate flowers in spaced whorls on terminal spikes.
- **Fruit/harvest:** Four nutlets.

## Renderer construction rules

Use sage architecture and leaf texture; treat purple foliage as cultivar colour overlay, not a different leaf shape.
