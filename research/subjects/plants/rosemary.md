# Rosemary (Salvia rosmarinus)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Evergreen woody shrub with upright or arching branches.
- **Leaves:** Opposite narrow linear leaves, revolute margins, dark green above and pale tomentose underside.
- **Stem and branching:** Woody stems branch repeatedly; young green shoots carry dense opposite leaves.
- **Flowers:** Small blue to violet bilabiate flowers in axillary clusters along upper stems.
- **Fruit/harvest:** Four small nutlets; no fleshy fruit.

## Renderer construction rules

Build a woody shrub with narrow needle-like opposite leaves and pale undersides; flowers sit in leaf axils, not terminal composite heads.
