# Garden sage (Salvia officinalis)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Evergreen to semi-evergreen subshrub, woody base with upright herbaceous flowering shoots.
- **Leaves:** Opposite oblong-ovate grey-green leaves, rugose surface, fine hairs and crenulate margins.
- **Stem and branching:** Square stems, opposite leaves, basal woody branching; flowering stems elongate above foliage.
- **Flowers:** Blue-violet bilabiate flowers in spaced whorls along terminal spikes.
- **Fruit/harvest:** Four nutlets within persistent calyx.

## Renderer construction rules

Render felted, wrinkled grey-green opposite leaves on square stems; flower whorls spaced along vertical spikes.
