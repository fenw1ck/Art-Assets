# Kale (Brassica oleracea Acephala Group)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Upright leafy biennial; edible leaves remain along an elongating central stem rather than forming a compact head.
- **Leaves:** Alternate simple leaves, broad ovate to oblong, blue-green or green, often waxy; margins range from wavy to deeply curled or serrated; prominent pale midrib.
- **Stem and branching:** Strong upright central stem with leaves attached at successive nodes; flowering in the second year produces an elongated branched flowering axis.
- **Flowers:** Yellow four-petalled cruciform flowers in loose racemes on the second-year flowering stem.
- **Fruit or harvest organ:** Long narrow siliques containing seeds; not a fleshy fruit.
