# Sweet potato (Ipomoea batatas)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Trailing or climbing perennial vine cultivated as an annual; storage roots form underground.
- **Leaves:** Alternate simple leaves highly variable from entire heart-shaped to deeply lobed; long petioles.
- **Stem and branching:** Long slender trailing stems root at nodes; storage roots arise from adventitious roots, not tubers on stolons.
- **Flowers:** Funnel-shaped morning-glory flowers, often pale lavender, uncommon in some cultivars.
- **Fruit/harvest:** Fleshy storage roots with smooth skin; not true tubers.

Distinguish from potato: trailing vine, variable entire/lobed leaves, adventitious storage roots; do not show potato compound leaves or stolon tubers.
