# German chamomile (Matricaria chamomilla)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Annual aromatic herb with erect, branching, finely divided stems.
- **Leaves:** Alternate leaves 2–3 pinnatisect into threadlike segments.
- **Stem and branching:** Slender stems branch repeatedly; terminal flower heads on long peduncles.
- **Flowers:** Small capitula with white rays around yellow conical disc; receptacle becomes hollow at maturity.
- **Fruit/harvest:** Small ribbed cypsela without showy pappus.

## Renderer construction rules

Use very finely dissected threadlike foliage and small daisy heads; avoid broad leaves and oversized flower heads.
