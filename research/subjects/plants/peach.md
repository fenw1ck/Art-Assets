# Peach (Prunus persica)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Deciduous fruit tree commonly trained open-vase with several main scaffold limbs and an open centre.
- **Leaves:** Alternate simple narrow lanceolate to elliptic-lanceolate leaves with long pointed tips, fine serrated margins and pinnate venation; short petioles may bear glands.
- **Stem and branching:** Slender reddish or green current-year shoots; fruiting wood is primarily one-year-old shoots. Branching should create an open vase, not a single dense central leader.
- **Flowers:** Pink flowers, usually solitary or in pairs, often appearing before leaves emerge; short pedicels on previous-season wood.
- **Fruit:** Fuzzy-skinned fleshy drupe with a hard central stone; nectarine cultivars are smooth-skinned.

## Renderer construction rules

Construct an open-vase tree with multiple outward scaffold limbs. Place pink flowers singly or in pairs directly on bare one-year wood before leaf-out. Later show narrow serrated leaves and peaches on current-season growth arising from that wood. Do not use apple-like spur clusters.
