# Leaf lettuce (Lactuca sativa (leaf-lettuce cultivars))

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Annual or biennial herb forming a low basal rosette; an erect flowering stem develops when bolting.
- **Leaves:** Basal leaves are soft, broad, variable from entire to lobed or frilled; lower leaves petiolate, upper stem leaves become smaller and sessile or clasping.
- **Stem and branching:** Short compressed stem in vegetative rosette; at bolting, central stem elongates and branches into a many-headed panicle.
- **Flowers:** Numerous small yellow Asteraceae capitula in dense corymbiform or paniculate arrays; each head contains multiple florets.
- **Fruit or harvest organ:** Small ribbed achene (cypsela) with a slender beak and white pappus; no fleshy fruit.

## Renderer construction rules

Vegetative stage is a layered basal rosette with overlapping leaves, not a leafy upright stalk. For bolting, elongate one central stem and branch it into many small yellow heads. Preserve the leaf form of the named leaf cultivar.
