# Cabbage (Brassica oleracea Capitata Group)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Biennial crop usually harvested in the first year as a compact head formed by overlapping leaves around a shortened stem.
- **Leaves:** Large broad smooth or savoyed leaves; outer rosette leaves spread outward, while inner leaves curve inward and overlap tightly to form the head.
- **Stem and branching:** Stem base is greatly shortened; closely spaced leaf nodes produce the compact head. In the second year a central flowering stem elongates.
- **Flowers:** Second-year open racemes of yellow four-petalled cruciform flowers; the edible head itself is vegetative leaf tissue, not a flower.
- **Fruit or harvest organ:** Narrow siliques after flowering.

## Renderer construction rules

Model head formation as successive leaves with increasing inward curvature around the apical meristem. Include larger outer wrapper leaves and compact inner folded leaves. Do not draw a broccoli-like bud cluster. Flower stalk belongs only to a bolted second-year plant.
