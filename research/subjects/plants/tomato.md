# Tomato

## Distinguishing features

- **Stem:** green and slightly hairy.
- **Leaves:** compound, alternating up the stem. Each has pairs of ovate, toothed, puckered leaflets, small leaflets between the pairs, and one leaflet at the tip.
- **Trusses** (flower and fruit clusters) grow between the nodes, on alternating sides. Each holds 3–7 fruit, or 7–16 for cherry tomatoes. Earlier in the season they hold yellow flowers.
- **Suckers:** a sucker grows in the angle between each leaf and the stem and repeats the whole plant at a smaller size. This is the plant's self-repeating pattern.
- **Determinate vs indeterminate:** determinate plants stop in a truss and stay bushy. Indeterminate vines keep climbing and are staked.
- **Fruit:** green, then orange, then red, oldest (lowest) first. A five-pointed green calyx sits on top.

## Construction order

Stem path, then suckers (behind), then the stem stroke, then leaves at the nodes, then the curled tip, then trusses every third node.
