# Watermelon (Citrullus lanatus)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Trailing annual vine with long prostrate runners.
- **Leaves:** Alternate deeply pinnatifid leaves, lobes broad and rounded, rough surface.
- **Stem and branching:** Long creeping stems branch at nodes; tendrils usually simple or 2-fid; male and female flowers solitary in leaf axils.
- **Flowers:** Yellow five-parted unisexual flowers; female flowers have inferior ovary visible behind corolla.
- **Fruit/harvest:** Large pepo with hard rind and fleshy interior.

## Renderer construction rules

Show prostrate runners, deeply lobed leaves and axillary tendrils; fruit is a large ground-level pepo, not hanging berry.
