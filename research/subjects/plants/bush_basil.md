# Bush basil (Ocimum basilicum (compact cultivar group))

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Compact bushy annual herb with many short lateral shoots.
- **Leaves:** Opposite simple ovate leaves, aromatic, entire to slightly toothed.
- **Stem and branching:** Repeated opposite branching creates dense rounded crown; flowering spikes terminal.
- **Flowers:** Small white to pink bilabiate flowers in whorls on terminal racemes.
- **Fruit/harvest:** Four nutlets in persistent calyx.

## Renderer construction rules

Retain basil opposite leaves but construct a dense rounded bush with short internodes and repeated lateral branching.
