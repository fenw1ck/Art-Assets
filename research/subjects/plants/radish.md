# Radish (Raphanus sativus)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Annual or biennial root crop with low basal rosette; flowering plants produce an erect branched stem.
- **Leaves:** Rough lyrate-pinnate basal leaves with a large terminal lobe and smaller lateral lobes; upper stem leaves become smaller and simpler.
- **Stem and branching:** Short crown and swollen taproot in the edible stage; a reddish or green flowering stem elongates from the crown when bolting.
- **Flowers:** Four-petalled white, pink or mauve flowers in elongated racemes; typical Brassicaceae cross shape.
- **Fruit or harvest organ:** Elongated silique, often swollen or somewhat constricted around seeds; edible radish is a tuberous taproot, not fruit.

## Renderer construction rules

Draw the root as a swollen napiform, cylindrical or tapered storage root with a fine lower taproot. Leaf rosette should be lyrate, not simple oval leaves. In flowering stage, add an erect raceme and elongated seed pods.
