# Cauliflower (Brassica oleracea Botrytis Group)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Biennial crop forming compact immature inflorescence curd surrounded by wrapper leaves.
- **Leaves:** Large alternate waxy leaves, broad oblong, often upright and arching around curd.
- **Stem and branching:** Short stem with tightly clustered proliferating flower meristems forming dense curd; later elongates into flowering stem.
- **Flowers:** Edible curd is undeveloped inflorescence; if bolted, yellow cruciform flowers in racemes.
- **Fruit/harvest:** Siliques after flowering.

## Renderer construction rules

Construct dense ivory/coloured granular curd protected by large wrapper leaves; distinguish from broccoli's separate green bud branches.
