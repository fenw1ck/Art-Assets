# European pear (dwarf) (Pyrus communis)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Deciduous fruit tree; dwarf form is maintained by rootstock and pruning. Commonly upright central leader with scaffold branches.
- **Leaves:** Alternate simple glossy ovate to round-ovate leaves, tapering to a point; margins subtly toothed to crenate; petioles distinct.
- **Stem and branching:** Woody trunk and upright scaffold branches; numerous short fruiting spurs on older wood. Branches often more upright than apple depending on cultivar and training.
- **Flowers:** White five-petalled blossoms in clusters (corymbs) on short spurs; bloom early in spring.
- **Fruit:** Pome, characteristically pear-shaped with a narrow neck and broader lower portion; green to yellow-brown, sometimes blushed.

## Renderer construction rules

Build a woody central leader with upright scaffold branches and short fruit spurs. Keep leaves simple and glossy, not compound. Put white blossom clusters and later pears on short spurs. Avoid apple's round fruit silhouette.
