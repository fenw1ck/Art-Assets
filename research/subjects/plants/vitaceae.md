# Grape family

## Parts

- Woody climbing vines. **Tendrils** grow opposite the leaves and coil round supports (in Virginia creeper they end in sticky discs instead).
- Flowers small and greenish, in clusters opposite the leaves. Fruit a berry.
- Muscadine and Virginia creeper belong here (see their entries).
