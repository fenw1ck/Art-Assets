# Turnip (Brassica rapa subsp. rapa)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Annual or biennial root crop with basal rosette; flowering stem elongates in second year.
- **Leaves:** Lower leaves lyrate and bristly, upper stem leaves smaller and clasping.
- **Stem and branching:** Short crown with swollen hypocotyl/taproot; bolting stem branches into flowering racemes.
- **Flowers:** Yellow four-petalled cruciform flowers in racemes.
- **Fruit/harvest:** Swollen storage hypocotyl/root; narrow siliques after flowering.

## Renderer construction rules

Render swollen root with basal lyrate leaves; distinguish from radish by leaf/root profile and yellow flowering racemes.
