# Peace lily (Spathiphyllum wallisii (species reference; cultivated hybrids vary))

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Evergreen rhizomatous herb forming a dense basal clump.
- **Leaves:** Glossy dark-green simple lanceolate to oblong-elliptic leaves with long petioles, pointed tips and prominent midrib; leaves arise from crown.
- **Stem and branching:** Short basal rhizome; individual petioles emerge from crown. Flowering stalks rise separately from foliage and may exceed leaf height.
- **Flowers:** A single white or cream spathe (modified bract) encloses a short upright cream spadix covered in tiny flowers; spathe greens with age.
- **Fruit or propagules:** Fruit not usually a visible ornamental feature; species-level fruit details are not required for standard houseplant image.

## Renderer construction rules

Build a basal clump of long-petioled lanceolate leaves. Each flower stalk carries one white spathe around one central spadix. Do not depict a daisy-like flower or a many-flowered branched inflorescence.
