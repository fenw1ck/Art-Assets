# White eggplant (Solanum melongena (white-fruited cultivar group))

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Warm-season branching perennial grown as annual; upright bushy architecture.
- **Leaves:** Alternate large ovate leaves, often slightly lobed, soft-hairy; prominent veins.
- **Stem and branching:** Branching forked stems; flowers and fruits arise at branch forks or leaf axils.
- **Flowers:** Purple star-shaped flowers with yellow anthers; sometimes white/pale lavender.
- **Fruit/harvest:** Large berry, white in target cultivar group; calyx persists around fruit shoulder.
