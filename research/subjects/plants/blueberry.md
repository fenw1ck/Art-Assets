# Highbush blueberry (Vaccinium corymbosum)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Woody, multi-stemmed deciduous shrub with upright and arching branches.
- **Leaves:** Alternate simple elliptical leaves, entire to finely serrated; green above, often paler or slightly hairy beneath; fine venation visible.
- **Stem and branching:** Several woody stems arise from crown; young twigs may be green or reddish and slightly zigzag. Flower buds are rounded and plumper than vegetative buds.
- **Flowers:** Small urn- or bell-shaped white to pinkish flowers in drooping clusters (corymbs) on short lateral shoots.
- **Fruit or propagules:** Round blue berries with a conspicuous powdery wax bloom; persistent calyx at distal end.

## Renderer construction rules

Construct a woody multi-stem shrub with fine twig branching, not a herbaceous bush. Place flower clusters and fruit clusters on short lateral shoots. Use alternate elliptical leaves and preserve the blue-grey bloom on ripe berries.
