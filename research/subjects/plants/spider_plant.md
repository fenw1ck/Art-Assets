# Spider plant (Chlorophytum comosum)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Tufted perennial herb forming a basal rosette; long arching to pendulous inflorescence stolons carry flowers and plantlets.
- **Leaves:** Narrow linear to linear-lanceolate strap leaves, arching or recurved, smooth, with parallel veins; green or longitudinally variegated by cultivar.
- **Stem and branching:** Short rhizome and basal crown; multiple leaves emerge from rosette. Long slender stalks arch outward and branch or carry plantlets at nodes/bracts.
- **Flowers:** Small white star-shaped flowers along lax arching inflorescence, borne in small clusters.
- **Fruit or propagules:** Small three-angled capsule; vegetative plantlets commonly form along the inflorescence and root when they touch soil.

## Renderer construction rules

Make a fountain-like basal rosette of narrow arching leaves. Add long thin arching runners/inflorescence stems extending beyond foliage; place tiny white flowers and daughter plantlets along them. Plantlets are not berries or flowers.

## Research gaps

- Cultivar variegation pattern
- Plantlet density and arching-stolon length for target specimen
