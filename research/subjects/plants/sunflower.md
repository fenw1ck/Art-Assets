# Sunflower (Helianthus annuus)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Coarse erect annual, usually with one dominant upright stem; some cultivars branch and carry multiple heads.
- **Leaves:** Lower leaves may be opposite; upper leaves alternate. Simple broad ovate to cordate blades, rough/hispid on both surfaces, toothed margins, long petioles and prominent basal veins.
- **Stem and branching:** Stout rough-hairy stem; typically one large terminal head in single-stem cultivars, with branches and smaller heads in branching cultivars.
- **Flowers:** Large terminal capitulum: outer yellow ray florets surround a central disc of numerous small tubular fertile florets; surrounded by green involucral bracts.
- **Fruit:** Dry achenes (sunflower seeds) develop from disc florets across the central receptacle; not a fleshy fruit.

## Renderer construction rules

Build a stout upright stem with broad rough alternate leaves (lower opposite leaves may be shown). Place the main head at the apex. Construct a ring of ray florets around a dense disc with visible spiral seed pattern as it matures. Branching is cultivar-dependent.
