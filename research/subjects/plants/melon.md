# Muskmelon (Cucumis melo)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Trailing or climbing annual vine, often with lateral branches.
- **Leaves:** Alternate simple rounded-cordate leaves, shallowly lobed and rough.
- **Stem and branching:** Vine branches from nodes with simple tendrils; male flowers commonly in clusters, female flowers solitary in axils.
- **Flowers:** Yellow unisexual flowers; female flower has inferior ovary.
- **Fruit/harvest:** Pepo with netted or smooth rind depending on cultivar.

## Renderer construction rules

Use rounded shallow-lobed cucurbit leaves; separate clustered male blooms from solitary female blooms and place fruit at female nodes.
