# Pothos (Epipremnum aureum)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Climbing or trailing evergreen hemiepiphyte; juvenile indoor stems trail, while mature plants climb supports and develop much larger leaves.
- **Leaves:** Alternate simple leaves; juvenile blades entire, waxy and heart-shaped. Mature blades become much larger and may develop deep lobes or divisions; variegation is cultivar-dependent.
- **Stem and branching:** Long jointed stems with conspicuous nodes and long internodes; aerial roots emerge at nodes and attach to support. Branching sparse; stems may trail or climb.
- **Flowers:** Araceae spathe-and-spadix inflorescence is rarely produced in cultivation; not a feature of typical juvenile houseplant rendering.
- **Fruit or propagules:** If pollinated, berry-like fruits form along the spadix; generally absent in houseplant specimens.

## Renderer construction rules

Choose juvenile houseplant form unless a mature climbing specimen is explicitly requested. Juvenile leaves remain entire and heart-shaped; do not add Monstera fenestrations. Show node spacing and occasional aerial roots. A mature wild-type stage may enlarge and divide leaves.
