# Borage (Borago officinalis)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Coarse annual herb with erect branching stems covered in stiff hairs.
- **Leaves:** Alternate simple ovate leaves, rough and bristly; lower leaves petiolate, upper leaves sessile.
- **Stem and branching:** Branching stems bear nodding flower clusters; coiled scorpioid cymes characteristic of Boraginaceae.
- **Flowers:** Star-shaped blue flowers with five pointed petals and prominent dark anther cone.
- **Fruit/harvest:** Four nutlets.
