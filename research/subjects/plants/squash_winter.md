# Winter squash (Cucurbita spp.; profile representative of Cucurbita maxima types)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Long trailing annual vine with vigorous runners; some cultivar groups climb or sprawl.
- **Leaves:** Large alternate cordate to orbicular-cordate leaves, usually shallowly lobed, rough and hairy on both surfaces; long stout petioles.
- **Stem and branching:** Robust prostrate stems with coiling, branched tendrils at nodes. Cucurbita maxima stems can be softer and more rounded than the hard, angled stems of C. pepo.
- **Flowers:** Large solitary yellow unisexual flowers; male and female flowers on the same plant. Female flower has a swollen ovary below the corolla.
- **Fruit:** Large pepo with highly variable shape, rind and colour; mature fruit is borne at a female-flower node and has a stout, often flared peduncle.

## Renderer construction rules

Render as a long ground-spreading vine, not a compact zucchini bush. Give leaves broad, nearly round cordate blades with shallow lobes and pale mottling only if cultivar supports it. Add branched tendrils. Place one or a few large fruits at female-flower nodes.
