# Garden asparagus (Asparagus officinalis)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Perennial crown with underground rhizome; spring edible spears mature into tall, finely branched fern-like shoots.
- **Leaves:** True leaves are tiny papery scales; green needle-like structures are cladodes (modified branchlets) in clusters at nodes, not broad leaves.
- **Stem and branching:** Several upright spears emerge from a crown. Mature stems branch repeatedly in the upper portion, with fine threadlike cladodes arranged in fascicles.
- **Flowers:** Small yellowish-green bell-shaped flowers in axillary racemes; plants are dioecious or bear unisexual flowers.
- **Fruit or harvest organ:** Small red berries on female plants.

## Renderer construction rules

Distinguish the harvest spear from mature fern foliage. Mature form must be airy and finely branched, with bundles of slender cladodes along branchlets; never use broad leaves. Place small flowers and red berries at axillary positions.
