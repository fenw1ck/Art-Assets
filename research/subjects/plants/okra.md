# Okra (Abelmoschus esculentus)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Tall erect annual herb, branched with coarse stems.
- **Leaves:** Alternate palmately lobed leaves, 3–7 lobes, rough texture and long petioles.
- **Stem and branching:** Strong upright stem with alternate leaves; axillary flower and fruit stalks.
- **Flowers:** Large solitary yellow hibiscus-like flower with dark maroon centre.
- **Fruit/harvest:** Elongated angular ridged capsule, upright or pendent depending cultivar.

## Renderer construction rules

Use alternate palmately lobed leaves and solitary hibiscus-like axillary blooms; fruit is a long ridged capsule, not pod of legumes.
