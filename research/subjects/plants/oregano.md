# Oregano (Origanum vulgare)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Perennial aromatic herb, spreading clump with upright square stems; may become woody at base.
- **Leaves:** Opposite simple ovate leaves, entire to shallowly toothed, aromatic glandular surface.
- **Stem and branching:** Opposite leaves at nodes; stems branch from lower and middle axils; flowering shoots rise above foliage.
- **Flowers:** Tiny pinkish-purple or white bilabiate flowers in dense terminal corymb-like clusters/spikelets.
- **Fruit/harvest:** Small dry nutlets; aromatic leaves are harvested.

## Renderer construction rules

Use opposite paired leaves and square branching stems; flowers are many tiny bilabiate florets in terminal clusters, not single daisy heads.
