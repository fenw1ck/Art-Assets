# Fuchsia (*Fuchsia magellanica*)

## Distinguishing features
- Bushy shrub with opposite or whorled leaves, depending on species and node.
- Flowers are usually pendent and distinctive: a conspicuous tubular calyx, four spreading sepals, and four more erect petals inside. The projecting stamens/style may hang below the corolla.
- Flowers are usually solitary in leaf axils, each on a slender drooping pedicel.
- Red, pink and purple combinations are common in cultivated forms.

## Construction rules
1. Build a branched woody/herbaceous shrub framework with paired or whorled leaves.
2. Place pendent axillary flower stalks at nodes; flowers hang below the foliage.
3. Model the elongated tubular calyx and four sepals separately from the inner four petals. Add projecting stamens/style.
4. Immature: leafy branches with hanging buds. Mature: numerous pendent flowers. Past flowering: reduce open flowers and retain some calyx/fruit stalks and foliage; do not replace with daisy-like heads.
5. Seed branch architecture, leaf size, pedicel length and flower colour while preserving pendent structure.
