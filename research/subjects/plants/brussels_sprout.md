# Brussels sprouts (Brassica oleracea Gemmifera Group)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Tall upright biennial stem with many compact axillary leaf buds along its length.
- **Leaves:** Large alternate cabbage-like leaves with long petioles and broad blades; leaf rosette crowns top.
- **Stem and branching:** Strong central stem with tightly spaced nodes; miniature cabbage heads develop in leaf axils along stem.
- **Flowers:** Second-year yellow cruciform flowers on elongated racemes.
- **Fruit/harvest:** Narrow siliques after flowering; edible sprouts are axillary buds.

## Renderer construction rules

Show tall stem with repeated small round sprouts in leaf axils, plus large leaves above and below; not a ground-level cabbage head.
