# Garden strawberry (Fragaria × ananassa)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Low perennial crown with basal rosette; spreads laterally by long stolons that root and form daughter rosettes.
- **Leaves:** Alternate trifoliate compound leaves; three serrated leaflets, often softly hairy, on long petioles.
- **Stem and branching:** Very short compressed crown; runners (stolons) extend horizontally and produce plantlets at nodes. Flower stalks arise separately from crown.
- **Flowers:** Small clusters of white five-petalled flowers with yellow centres; numerous stamens and many separate carpels on a raised receptacle.
- **Fruit or propagules:** Accessory aggregate fruit: fleshy enlarged receptacle with many dry achenes embedded on its outer surface.

## Renderer construction rules

Build a compact low crown, not a branched upright stem. Show three serrated leaflets per leaf. Add thin horizontal runners with daughter rosettes at their nodes. Place white flower clusters on separate stalks from crown; red berries carry visible surface achenes.
