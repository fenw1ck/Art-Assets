# Individual research-gap audit — 25 November 2026

Scope: every entry in `plant-art/infocards.json` `research_gaps` was audited individually. This file distinguishes facts supported at species level from choices that cannot be resolved without a named cultivar, rootstock, specimen stage or visual reference. A generic database landing page is never counted as evidence.

Status meanings:
- `requires_user_selection`: cannot be settled by research alone; the user must choose cultivar/rootstock/stage.
- `visual_reference_needed`: needs an identified image for the specified state.
- `source_recorded`: links exist in the research record, but claim-level verification is still required.
- `source_verification_needed`: no taxon-specific source is recorded.
- `species_scope_or_rendering_issue`: clarify taxon scope or correct the rendering requirement.
- `species_level_evidence_recorded`: retain species-level baseline; source claim-level verification remains required.
- `species_level_reference_needed`: obtain direct evidence for the specific trait.

These are individual dispositions, not a claim that every gap is closed. Rendering remains prohibited for the first 57 infocards.
