# Pepper and eggplant

## Distinguishing features

- **Leaves:** simple, alternate and ovate, with a pointed tip and a smooth edge. Pepper leaves are glossy. Eggplant leaves are larger, grey-green and wavy-edged.
- **Branching:** the stem grows a few leaves, then forks. One flower (later one fruit) hangs at each fork, and two, sometimes three, branches go on to fork again. This is the self-repeating pattern.
- **Flowers:** pepper flowers are white and star-shaped with five to seven petals. Eggplant flowers are purple.
- **Fruit:** a bell pepper hangs down, blocky, with three or four lobes. It ripens from green to orange and red, the oldest (lowest) first. An eggplant is a long, glossy purple teardrop under a spiky green cap.
