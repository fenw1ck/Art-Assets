# Celery (Apium graveolens)

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Biennial herb cultivated for thick leaf stalks; basal rosette, then tall flowering stem.
- **Leaves:** Pinnate leaves with toothed, lobed leaflets; broad grooved petioles form edible stalks.
- **Stem and branching:** Short crown with leaves arising in basal rosette; second-year branched flowering stems.
- **Flowers:** Small white flowers in compound umbels with many rays.
- **Fruit/harvest:** Small dry schizocarp splitting into mericarps; edible stalk is petiole.

## Renderer construction rules

Emphasize broad ribbed/grooved petioles and pinnate leaves; umbels are compound, not single flower heads.
