# Garden cosmos (*Cosmos bipinnatus*)

## Distinguishing features
- Tall, open, branching annual with slender upright stems.
- Opposite, often decussate leaves, finely 1–3-pinnately dissected into narrow linear/filiform segments. Foliage should read as airy and feathery, not broad-leaved.
- Long peduncles carry terminal daisy-like composite heads, often in loose cymose arrays. Heads have broad pink/white ray florets around a yellow disc.
- Each head is one inflorescence composed of many florets; it is not a cluster of separate flowers.

## Construction rules
1. Build a slender upright framework with paired branching at nodes; maintain an open, airy silhouette.
2. Draw opposite finely divided leaves with narrow threadlike lobes.
3. Put solitary composite heads at ends of long peduncles, sometimes several heads in a loose terminal array.
4. Immature: fine foliage and buds, no open heads. Mature: open heads at varied heights. Past flowering: fade/drop rays and retain some dry heads and fine foliage.
5. Seed variation in branching, internode lengths, leaf dissection and head count; preserve the airy architecture.
