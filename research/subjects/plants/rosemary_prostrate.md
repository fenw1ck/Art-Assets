# Prostrate rosemary (Salvia rosmarinus (prostrate cultivar group))

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Low trailing woody evergreen shrub with cascading branches.
- **Leaves:** Opposite narrow linear revolute leaves, dark above and pale beneath.
- **Stem and branching:** Woody stems spread and cascade; short lateral shoots carry dense leaves.
- **Flowers:** Blue-violet bilabiate flowers in axillary clusters.
- **Fruit/harvest:** Four nutlets.

## Renderer construction rules

Keep the same rosemary leaf morphology but change architecture to a low cascading mound; never make it an upright herbaceous spike.
