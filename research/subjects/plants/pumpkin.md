# Pumpkin (Cucurbita pepo (pumpkin cultivar group))

## Distinguishing features

- **Growth architecture:** Robust trailing annual vine, often with long runners and large leaves.
- **Leaves:** Large alternate cordate to palmately lobed leaves, coarse hairs and toothed margins.
- **Stem and branching:** Thick angular stems with branching runners and branched tendrils; flowers solitary in leaf axils.
- **Flowers:** Large yellow-orange unisexual flowers; female flowers with conspicuous inferior ovary.
- **Fruit/harvest:** Large pepo with hard rind; fruit shape and ribbing cultivar-dependent.

## Renderer construction rules

Use thick rough runners, broad lobed leaves and branched tendrils; place large flowers and pumpkins at axillary nodes on separate short pedicels.
